五、可供当前使用的英文结果段落（仅陈述已完成的 9 例）

To investigate the underestimated LIM values, nine representative runs were
examined, with three runs selected from each analytical-reference model.
Replaying the saved queries reproduced the original classifications. Tracing
the decisive queries revealed that a grid cell could contain states with
different basin membership. A non-target label obtained from one trajectory
was therefore incorrectly inherited by a returning trajectory entering the
same cell. The misclassified trajectory then propagated the label to further
visited cells. Independent reintegration with ode45 and ode113, stricter
tolerances, and model-specific energy or escape criteria supported target
membership for the original underestimated decisive states. In a controlled
ablation, only reuse of non-target and domain-exit cell labels was disabled;
target-label reuse, numerical settings, search rules and random seeds were
retained. Underestimation disappeared in all nine selected runs, and the new
decisive states were independently supported as non-target. These results
identify cross-boundary cell-label reuse and subsequent propagation as the
mechanism responsible for the inspected failures. They do not establish
correctness of all cached labels or global accuracy of the returned LIM.

六、当前可给 Genda 的英文进度回复（不是最终修回完成声明）

Your concern about the underestimated LIM values is justified. I traced the
decisive initial states and the provenance of their learned grid labels in
nine representative two-dimensional runs. The failure occurred when a returning
trajectory inherited a non-target label from a different state in the same
grid cell. This incorrect classification was then propagated to additional
cells. Independent reintegration supported target membership for the affected
decisive states. Disabling only non-target and domain-exit label reuse removed
underestimation in all nine inspected runs, and both independent solvers
supported the new decisive states as non-target. I have prepared a paired
evaluation of all 30 original seeds for each of the three models to assess
the extent of this effect. That evaluation has not yet been completed. The
separable four-dimensional case and the unresolved-trajectory policy remain
separate checks; I do not consider those issues resolved by this result.

七、不可过度陈述的事项
- 原参考值不通过裁剪新结果被强行满足，不能写成事后将 LIM 修正到参考值。
- 本次证据针对所用分类实现，不能泛称 Habib 的整个 DynIn 算法错误。
- 原记录与 9 例溯源不支持把这里的直接原因归结为 automatic=1；也不能因此
  证明这个规则在低阻尼、实验 A/B 或其他模型上安全。
- 9 例代表案例有选择性，不能代替 90 次的总体失效率。
- 独立积分的最大允许时域为 2400/12000，但满足物理判据时会提前结束。
  必须报告实际积分长度/停止条件，不能声称所有轨迹积分到了 12000。
- 新结果略高于参考不等于严格安全下界；真实非目标点的距离只给出 LIM 上界。
- 本轮不以速度为论点。科学贡献和算法比较公平性是另外的问题。
